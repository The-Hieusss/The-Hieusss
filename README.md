## Featured Project

### [M.I.S.A.](https://github.com/The-Hieusss/misa): Mac Internal Services Assistant

A floating assistant that sits above every app on your Mac. Click her, press **⌥Space** or say **"Hey MISA"**, and she converts and moves files, finds things with Spotlight, runs focus timers, manages your Calendar and Reminders, controls music, windows and Control Center, and more. Everything runs on-device: Apple's on-device model understands plain English, with no account, API key or subscription. Her mecha halo is fully customizable, down to the eye and her robotic arms.

**Swift · AppKit · WebKit · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS**
